Our Story

From One Mat To A Full Gym

DoBu opened with a single karate class in a rented room. As members asked for more, the gym grew around them — judo, boxing, a full strength gym, and a sauna to recover in.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Discipline

Consistency beats intensity. We build habits on the mat that hold up outside the gym too.

Respect

For instructors, training partners, and yourself. It's the first thing we teach, before any technique.

Community

Members train alongside each other for years, not just sessions. You'll know names, not just faces.

Growth

Every belt and grading marks real progress. We track effort over time, not one good class.
